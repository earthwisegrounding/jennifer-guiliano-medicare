About me

I'm Jennifer Guiliano, a licensed, independent Medicare insurance agent based in the West Valley. I help people across the Valley make sense of Medicare — whether they're turning 65, retiring, or taking a fresh look at the coverage they already have.

Because I'm independent, I'm not tied to a single insurance company. I represent 17 — including Aetna, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Wellcare, and SCAN — so I can compare your options side by side and explain the differences.

Why I focus on Medicare

Medicare can feel overwhelming. The mail piles up, the enrollment windows have deadlines, and every plan looks a little different. My job is to slow things down, answer your questions clearly, and help you choose coverage you feel good about — and then be here when you need help later.

How I work with you

  • I listen first. Your doctors, prescriptions, budget, and priorities come before any plan.
  • I explain things clearly and simply and answer every question — there are no silly ones.
  • No pressure, ever. The decision is yours, and there's no obligation to enroll.
  • I'm here after you enroll for questions, billing issues, and a yearly review.

What "independent agent" means for you

I'm appointed with more than one insurance company, so I can explain options from several companies instead of just one. I'm paid by the insurance company when someone enrolls — you don't pay me anything, and a plan's premium is the same whether or not you use an agent.

I don't represent every plan available in every area. For information on all of your options, you can always contact Medicare.gov, 1-800-MEDICARE, or Arizona's State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP).
